### 4. The scanner layout bug (BUGS.md #5) — the hard part
The Confirm button / result bar at the bottom of the full-screen scanner was pushed off-screen on
iPhone Safari. Two real fixes plus a workaround:
- **Fix 1 (`9a83659`)**: `.viewfinder-camera` had both `height:100%` and `flex:1` (conflicting),
  forcing full-viewport height. Removed `height:100%`, kept `flex:1 + min-height:0`. Improved but
  still not reachable.
- **Fix 2 (`7bd1caf`)**: size the viewfinder to `window.visualViewport.height` on open + resize
  (iOS Safari's bottom toolbar overlays fixed elements). dvh/vh as CSS fallback.
- **Workaround (`758f15e`)**: switched to auto-lookup so the Confirm button is **no longer needed**.
  This is why the scanner "works" now — we removed the dependency on the unreachable control.

## Honest notes on the scanner — what failed and what's still fragile

**The scanner works, but underneath it is held together loosely. Do not assume it is robust.**

1. **The layout bug was never definitively fixed — it was bypassed.** The result bar + green Confirm
   button are still likely partially off-screen on iOS Safari; auto-lookup just means we don't rely
   on them. That markup (`#barcode-result`, `#barcode-confirm-btn`) is now effectively **vestigial**
   — a fallback that may be unreachable. A proper fix would anchor the controls as an
   absolutely-positioned overlay pinned to the bottom of the visible area, so viewport/toolbar
   changes can't hide them. Worth doing before relying on any on-screen scanner control again.
2. **iOS `position: fixed` + the disappearing bottom toolbar is genuinely finicky.** `100vh`,
   `100dvh`, and `visualViewport.height` each measure subtly different things depending on scroll
   state, and the toolbar overlays fixed content rather than shrinking it. The `visualViewport`
   sizing (Fix 2) is the most reliable approach but was never cleanly verified on-device.
3. **Debugging was slow for two avoidable reasons — learn from this:**
   - I never asked for a **screenshot** early. For an iOS-only layout bug this is the single highest
     -value diagnostic (per the Bug #1 lesson in CLAUDE.md) and I skipped straight to fixes, guessing
     at a layout I couldn't see.
   - **Service-worker caching poisoned the feedback loop.** Each fix needed a fresh load, but iOS
     Safari + the SW cache hold old assets hard, so it was unclear whether Ali was testing the new
     fix or a stale build. I was "fixing blind" and getting ambiguous signals. Bumping
     `CACHE_VERSION` every deploy helps but isn't a guarantee on iOS; a hard tab-close/reopen was
     needed each time.
4. **Camera-open reliability on iOS was never root-caused.** It failed once, then worked (probably a
   permission grant). If it flakes again, suspect the `getUserMedia → video.play()` gesture timing.
5. **Open Food Facts coverage is partial.** Many packaged goods are present, but local/store-brand
   items and missing `serving_quantity`/macros are common → the manual-add fallback matters and will
   be used often.

- **All food macros are per-gram (or per-unit) across the app.** The app ×100 to get per-100g.

- **`gh` is read-only** — the agent reads issues but cannot open/close them; `closes #N` in commits
  on `main` is what closes issues (GitHub server-side).
- **Feature work is branched then merged to `main`.** Many small branches this session; all merged.

## Current challenges and anticipated risks

- **Scanner on-screen controls are unreliable on iOS** (see above). Auto-lookup avoids them, but any
  future scanner UI (e.g. a manual "retry"/"cancel" inside the viewfinder) needs the overlay-anchored
  approach, not flex children.
- **No automated tests.** All verification is manual/on-device, which is exactly why the iOS bugs
  were slow. A few unit tests around `calcFoodNutrition` and the per-gram/per-unit normalization
  would pay off as food logic grows.
- **Multi-user data still unsecured** (no RLS, #10/#11). `user_foods` and `food_logs` rely on
  client-side `user_id` filters only.
- **Open Food Facts is a third-party dependency** with partial coverage and no SLA; the app must keep
  degrading gracefully to manual entry.
